# Supporting Biological References — BLA

### Abatis et al. 2017 — PN→PN connectivity in BLA

> Abatis M, Niu R, Perin R, Giobellina G, Bito H, Markram H, Stoop R.
> **"The role of local excitatory networks in the lateral amygdala in emotional memory learning."**
> *Society for Neuroscience annual meeting, poster*, Washington DC, 2017.

**Status (resolved Oct 2026):** Feng’s reference list shows this is a **conference poster**, not a journal article, so no DOI/PMID exists. The distance-dependent probabilities (3 % <50 µm, 2 % 50–100 µm, 1 % 100–200 µm, 0.5 % 200–600 µm) are published only through Feng et al. 2019 Tables 5–6. In the thesis, cite **Feng et al. 2019** for the numbers and mention the unpublished poster as Feng’s basis. Note it as a limitation: these PN→PN values are not peer-reviewed as a standalone measurement.

### Samson & Paré 2006 — Spatially structured network in lateral amygdala

> Samson RD, Paré D.
> **"A spatially structured network of inhibitory and excitatory connections directs impulse traffic within the lateral amygdala"**
> *Neuroscience*, 141(3):1599–1609, 2006.
> DOI: 10.1016/j.neuroscience.2006.04.077 · PMID: 16753264

**Provides:** Evidence that principal-cell axons preferentially contact interneurons locally and other principal cells at longer distances — supporting distance-dependent and spatially structured recurrent connectivity inside the BLA.

---

### Rainnie et al. 1993 — BLA neuron electrophysiology

> Rainnie DG, Asprodini EK, Shinnick-Gallagher P.
> **"Intracellular recordings from morphologically identified neurons of the basolateral amygdala"**
> *Journal of Neurophysiology*, 69(4):1350–1362, 1993.
> DOI: 10.1152/jn.1993.69.4.1350 · PMID: 8492168

**Provides:** Electrophysiological characterization of adapting vs continuous-spiking principal cells and FSIs; basis for adaptation classification and sAHP magnitudes used in Kim/Feng models.

---

### Zador et al. 1990 — NMDA Mg²⁺ block

> Zador A, Koch C, Brown TH.
> **"Biophysical model of a Hebbian synapse"**
> *PNAS*, 87(17):6718–6722, 1990.
> DOI: 10.1073/pnas.87.17.6718

**Provides:** s(V) = [1 + 0.33 × exp(−0.06V)]⁻¹, the voltage-dependent Mg²⁺ block used in Feng et al.
