# General Computational Neuroscience References

### Hodgkin and Huxley 1952 — The foundational HH model

> Hodgkin AL, Huxley AF.
> **"A quantitative description of membrane current and its application to conduction and excitation in nerve"**
> *Journal of Physiology*, 117(4), 1952.

---

### Destexhe et al. 1994 — Dual-exponential synapse model

> Destexhe A, Mainen ZF, Sejnowski TJ.
> **"Synthesis of models for excitable membranes, synaptic transmission and neuromodulation using a common kinetic formalism"**
> *Journal of Computational Neuroscience*, 1994.

---

### Destexhe et al. 2001 — Point-conductance noise (methods source used by Feng)

> Destexhe A, Rudolph M, Fellous JM, Sejnowski TJ.
> **"Fluctuating synaptic conductances recreate in vivo-like activity in neocortical neurons"**
> *Neuroscience*, 107(1):13–24, 2001.
> DOI: 10.1016/S0306-4522(01)00344-X · PMID: 11744242

**This is the correct methods citation for the OU / point-conductance noise model.** Feng et al. 2019 cite “Destexhe et al., 2001” for the equations whose coefficients appear in Table 7.

---

### Destexhe et al. 2003 — High-conductance state (review)

> Destexhe A, Rudolph M, Paré D.
> **"The high-conductance state of neocortical neurons in vivo"**
> *Nature Reviews Neuroscience*, 4:739–751, 2003.

Conceptual background only. Different co-authors from the 2001 methods paper. Do not cite this as the source of Feng’s numerical noise parameters.

---

### Wang and Buzsáki 1996 — Interneuron-network gamma (ING)

> Wang XJ, Buzsáki G.
> **"Gamma oscillation by synaptic inhibition in a hippocampal interneuronal network model"**
> *Journal of Neuroscience*, 16(20), 1996.

Classic **ING** model. Feng’s BLA gamma is **PING** (PN↔FSI). Formal PING: Traub et al. 1997 / Whittington et al. 1997.
